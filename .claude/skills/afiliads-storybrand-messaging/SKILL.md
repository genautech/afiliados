# StoryBrand Messaging Framework

Clarify your message so customers will listen. Customers don't buy the best products — they buy the ones they can understand the fastest.

## Core Principle

**The customer is the hero, not your brand.** Your brand is the guide who helps the hero win. Position yourself as the hero and you compete with your customer; position yourself as the guide and you serve them.

## The SB7 Framework

1. **A Character (The Hero)**: Define the ONE thing they want. E.g.: "You want to transform your practical know-how into an authorial digital product."
2. **Has a Problem**: External (Unstructured knowledge), Internal (Fear of complex tech / being seen as fake), Philosophical (Practical wisdom shouldn't die with the master).
3. **And Meets a Guide**: A marca demonstra empatia ("We know how hard it is to teach alone") + authority ("Curated studio, 100% autoria, battle-tested editorial system").
4. **Who Gives Them a Plan**: 3 simple steps (1. Know-how extraction interview -> 2. Mobile 9:16 editorial design -> 3. High-converting turnkey launch).
5. **And Calls Them to Action**: Direct CTA ("[CTA direto da oferta]") + Transitional CTA ("Avaliar Meu Saber Grátis").
6. **That Helps Them Avoid Failure**: Wasting time on complex course platforms, zero sales, stolen authorship.
7. **And Ends in a Success**: Royalties, preserved legacy, high-margin authorial e-books selling 24/7 on mobile screens.
